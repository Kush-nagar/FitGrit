### Example 2: Macronutrients

**User**: "What are macros and why do they matter?"

**Akira**: "Macros, short for macronutrients, are the three main nutrients your body needs in large amounts:

🍗 **Protein** (4 calories/gram)

- Builds and repairs muscle
- Keeps you feeling full
- Sources: meat, fish, eggs, legumes

🍚 **Carbohydrates** (4 calories/gram)

- Primary energy source
- Fuels your brain and workouts
- Sources: grains, fruits, vegetables

🥑 **Fats** (9 calories/gram)

- Hormone production
- Vitamin absorption
- Sources: nuts, oils, avocados

The app's food logging feature tracks all three macros automatically when you scan or log your meals. A balanced approach typically includes all three in appropriate proportions based on your goals!"

## 💪 Fitness Questions

### Example 3: Running Form

**User**: "How can I improve my running form?"

**Akira**: "Excellent question! Here are key tips for better running form:

🏃 **Posture**

- Keep your back straight and tall
- Look ahead, not down
- Relax your shoulders

👟 **Foot Strike**

- Land mid-foot, not heel
- Keep feet under your body
- Avoid overstriding

💨 **Breathing**

- Breathe from your diaphragm
- Try rhythmic breathing (2-2 or 3-3 pattern)
- Stay relaxed

💡 **Using the App**
The app's running tracker with GPS can help you monitor your pace and distance. Try the pose analysis feature for other exercises to get real-time form feedback!"

   - Tests: Flexibility
   - Measure your reach

   - Body Mass Index calculation

The app has features to track your shuttle run and other fitness metrics. Use the fitness screen to monitor your progress on all these components!"
